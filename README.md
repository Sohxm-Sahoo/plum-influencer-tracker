# Influencer Analytics Scraper
A free, self-hosted Instagram and YouTube scraper built to track and analyze influencer ad collaborations at scale. 
It replaces manual tracking and paid SaaS tools with a single lightweight app that pulls the metrics that actually matter for marketing decisions.

## What it does
The tool collects post- and video-level performance data for **2000+ individual influencers**, including:

- View count
- Engagement (likes, comments, shares)
- Plays and unique viewers
- Retention rate
- Regional/audience breakdown

This data is used to evaluate how well a given influencer collaboration performed, and — 
more importantly — to gauge the market response to specific products or segments within the skincare lineup.

## Why it exists
Tracking hundreds of influencer campaigns manually is slow and error-prone,
and most third-party analytics platforms charge steep subscription fees for functionality that can be reproduced with a targeted scraper. 
This project automates that entire workflow, cutting SaaS spend by roughly **90%** while giving the team faster, more consistent access to campaign data.

## Status
Currently deployed and in private use at **Plum-Pureplay Skin Sciences**, where it supports ongoing influencer marketing analysis.
